# Design Direction: Claritas Class Ledger

Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Identity & Mood
A focused, reassuring, and intuitive SaaS financial environment tailored for modern educational institutions, class treasurers, teachers, and student representatives. The emotional target is immediate financial clarity, institutional trust, and effortless accountability.

## Visual Language
- **Style**: Clean Modern SaaS blended with accessible educational utility.
- **Palette**:
  - Primary: `#2563EB` (Actions, navigation, primary triggers)
  - Secondary: `#22C55E` (Inflow, paid status `Lunas`)
  - Warning / Accent: `#FACC15` (Pending, partial `Sebagian`)
  - Danger / Outflow: `#EF4444` (Arrears, expense, unpaid `Belum Lunas`)
  - Surface Background: `#F8FAFC`
  - Card Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `#E2E8F0`
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A`
  - Text Muted: `#64748B`
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (with `tabular-nums` for all financial ledger amounts).
- **Elevation**: Diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows with 1px border. No heavy drop shadows.
- **Shapes**: `16px` for cards, `10px`-`12px` for controls, full pill (`rounded-full`) for status badges.
- **Motion**: Functional hover states, state transitions, no unnecessary floating or template bounces.
